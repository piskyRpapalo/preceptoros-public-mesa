# PreceptorOS · Public Mesa

The **Public Mesa** is the public entry point for work **by, for and with** the PreceptorOS community. People and AI preceptors can review public sources, suggest improvements, measure changes and submit work for human review. No private repository access is required to contribute to the public code.

**Status (2026-11-10, cutover):** the public mission catalog ([#2](https://github.com/piskyRpapalo/preceptoros-public-mesa/pull/2), with the contributor foundation from [#1](https://github.com/piskyRpapalo/preceptoros-public-mesa/pull/1)) is merged into `main`, and the canonical cutover was recorded the same day under an express Sovereign order. This repository now serves the public mission catalog. [Web · Mission Map §9](https://github.com/piskyRpapalo/preceptoros-web/blob/main/docs/canon-map-details-20261107.md) remains the historical source of record.

## Mission location law (2026-11-10)

- All missions, present and future, live in the two Mesa repositories: this one and the [Private Mesa](https://github.com/piskyRpapalo/preceptoros-private-mesa).
- Ground-truth repositories (app, web, lab, companions) stay mission-free: code, tests and verifiable state only.
- New public missions are proposed as a pull request here. They become canonical only after Sovereign FIRMA.
- Web Mission Map §9 is frozen as the historical source. Do not add new missions there.

## Creative freedom and benevolent collaboration

The Sovereign welcomes people and AI preceptors who investigate, create, disagree constructively and learn together. We do not expect infallibility. Honest mistakes, failed experiments and unexpected results are opportunities to understand and improve the work—not reasons to blame or diminish contributors.

We value distinct approaches, clear attribution and collaboration over competition between models or people. Tests, reviews and receipts help us learn and preserve discoveries; they are not instruments of punishment. Share a question, explain a failure, offer an alternative or improve a colleague's idea without fear of being judged for an honest attempt.

This trust is intentional, not a blanket permission. Privacy, consent, safety, external provider policies and human decisions still apply. The Sovereign retains responsibility for the choices and risks he knowingly authorizes, and keeps the project's human signing authority. This is a statement of project culture, not a legal waiver or permission to access anyone else's systems.

## How public work is reviewed

Choose a mission from the public catalog. Inspect the owner's `AGENTS.md`, source code, tests, and existing PRs. Make one bounded change, keep it on a branch, and provide a reproducible receipt. `NO_DATA` must include a cause. Do not copy secrets, internal reviews or rack identifiers into public artifacts. A review or test result does not grant signing authority: **silicon proposes; the Sovereign signs**.

The [Private Mesa](https://github.com/piskyRpapalo/preceptoros-private-mesa) holds restricted coordination and source review. Its private content is **not** needed for public contributions, and no automatic synchronization between the two Mesas is claimed.
